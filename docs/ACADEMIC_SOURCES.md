# Academic verification record

Verification date: **2026-10-06**, using AUIS's official English website. Research began at [Academic Programs](https://www.auis.edu.krd/academic-programs) and followed its links to units, degrees, faculty, APP and the library. The editable facts live in `src/data/academics.js`; each cited fact group, program and faculty record includes its source URL and verification date.

The game is an original fantasy adaptation. Faculty roles, appearances, conversations and quests are fictional, while public academic names and specialties are factual summaries. Dialogue is not a quotation, personal biography or endorsement. Health activities use simulated samples and fictional ingredients.

## Department-count mismatch

The [Academic Programs overview](https://www.auis.edu.krd/academic-programs) says **eight departments** and two colleges, but links seven named departments and two colleges. The separate [Academic directory](https://www.auis.edu.krd/academic) repeats the same list. Those seven are Business Administration; Computing and Informatics; Engineering; English; Medical & Health Sciences; Social Sciences and Law; and Mathematics and Natural Sciences.

The official [2025–26 Academic Catalog](https://auis.edu.krd/sites/default/files/2025-09/Academic%20Catalog%2025-26%20UPDATED-.pdf), printed page 13 (PDF page index 12), explicitly says **seven departments and two colleges**. Its contents and unit sections corroborate seven academic departments. No reviewed current directory identifies an eighth linked academic department. The reason for the overview's eight-department count remains unresolved. The game represents the seven named departments and the two named colleges; APP remains a **preparatory program**, and the library remains a **library**. Neither is silently reclassified to reconcile the count.

Current web pages use Computing and Informatics and English; the catalog sometimes uses Information Technology and English and Translation. The game follows current unit-page labels and does not count the older labels as additional departments.

## Source uncertainties and display decisions

- The Engineering page's Artificial Intelligence and Robotics degree link resolves to the department page. The name is verified there and in the catalog; the game links to verified sources instead of inventing a detail URL. Catalog and progress-form naming sometimes adds “Engineering.”
- The English minor links also return to the department page. Their three names are visible there; the game links to that page.
- The research browser could not retrieve some detail pages on `www.auis.edu.krd`. The official non-`www` versions of the IT minor, Applied AI minor and Pharmacy degree were successfully reviewed. This retrieval issue alone does not establish that a public page is broken.
- Cybersecurity is explicitly listed by the current Computing and Informatics page and catalog, but its linked detail page could not be reviewed. The game claims its degree listing and links to the department, without adding unverified curriculum details.
- The Business department link says “Human Resource Management”; its degree page says “Human Resources Management.” The game uses the degree-page title and records the variation.
- The current Social Sciences and Law page describes two degrees but provides a detail link for International Relations. International Studies is confirmed by the same page and catalog. The catalog additionally lists Law and several minors; the current page's degree links are narrower. These additional catalog entries are not presented as a newly verified exhaustive current offering.
- Pharmacy naming varies between “Bachelor of Pharmacy,” “BSc. Pharm.” and “Bachelor Science of Pharmacy.” The detail page's degree label is used. Accreditation assertions, admissions thresholds, tuition, employment predictions and personal history are omitted because they are unnecessary for these activities.
- Empty `degrees` or `minors` arrays indicate that no corresponding offering from the selected current unit-page scope is displayed. They do not assert that AUIS offers none elsewhere. Recheck official pages before expanding this scope or updating a release.

To refresh facts, verify the relevant official page, update the text and URL in `src/data/academics.js`, advance that record's `verifiedAt`, and record any conflict here. Keep game dialogue and invented lore outside the factual records.

## Version 2 naming and visual scope

The nine academic region labels now use the exact official unit names already recorded above. The starting area displays Academic Preparatory Program, while the fictional Moulakis Archive keeps its name. The legacy data field `fantasy` repeats the official label for compatibility. Internal region, target and quest IDs remain unchanged.

This release changes visual design and interface behavior, without adding degree, curriculum, accreditation, faculty-biography or admissions claims. Existing official URLs and the original verification date remain intact. Buildings, equipment, clothing, faces, conversations and activities are imaginative teaching settings, not replicas of the AUIS campus or portraits of faculty. The overview/catalog department-count discrepancy remains unresolved and visible in the archive.
